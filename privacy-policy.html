Privacy Policy — Luck of the Deal

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Luck of the Deal ("the app") is a single-player poker game played with virtual, play-chips only — there is no real-money wagering, no purchase with real-world cash value, and nothing in the app can be cashed out. This policy explains what data the app does, and does not, collect.

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account creation, no sign-in, and no network connection required to play — the app works fully offline once installed.

All game data (your chip history, seat and roster preferences, and in-progress hand state used to resume after closing the app) is stored locally on your own device only. It is never transmitted to us, to the app developer, or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

Third-party services

The app does not currently integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is collected and why.

Children's privacy

This app depicts simulated gambling (play-chips poker) and is not directed at or marketed to children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to kidzi+luckofthedeal@idzifamily.com.
